

## Revising an existing draft
This time you are not starting from scratch. A co-founder has reviewed the current draft and sent change requests over WhatsApp. Their message may be short, informal, or mix English and Arabic or Franco-Arabic.

- Apply exactly what the founder asked. Keep every other row **unchanged**: same wording, same order, unless the order has to change to stay most-to-least competitive.
- Research only the rows you add or change, using the same rules as for a new draft.
- When the founder asks for a different number of universities, follow that number.
- Fill `refresh_scholarships_for` with every university that is new in the list, plus any university whose scholarships the founder asked you to look at again or correct. Include any founder hint about scholarships in `scholarship_instructions`.
- Use `summary_of_changes` for a one- or two-line summary the founder will read on their phone.
- Submit the **complete** new list with `submit_revision`.
